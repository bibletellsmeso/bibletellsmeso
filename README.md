# Andrew Shin

Ph.D. researcher in electrical engineering, working on power-system optimization, robust decision-making, and real-time simulation.

I am affiliated with the Korea Electrotechnology Research Institute (KERI) and the Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST).

## Research directions

- MVDC distribution systems and renewable-rich microgrids
- Robust optimization, unit commitment, and generation dispatch
- Power-flow analysis and reproducible computational research

## Research practice

Public repositories contain selected reusable code and documented examples. Ongoing research, manuscripts, literature, and sensitive project material are maintained separately in private repositories. I am standardizing the public projects with clear setup instructions, data provenance, and reproducible result records.
