# Andrew Shin

Ph.D. researcher in electrical engineering, working at the intersection of power-system optimization, robust decision-making, and real-time simulation.

- Research affiliations: Korea Electrotechnology Research Institute (KERI) and Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST)
- Current themes: MVDC distribution systems, renewable-rich microgrids, robust optimization, unit commitment, generation dispatch, and power-flow analysis
- Tools and methods: Python, mathematical programming, data analysis, and reproducible computational research
